Your Panda Lamp 🌟

Mi Osita Panda,

Today, on Teddy Day , I'm giving you something that I hope will remind you of me every single night — a Panda Lamp that glows softly in the dark, just like the love I carry for you even when we're apart.

I chose a panda because pandas are gentle, they're cuddly, and they look like they'd give the world's best hugs — just like you. And this one glows, because you are the light in my darkest moments. When everything feels overwhelming, you are the warm glow that tells me everything will be okay.

Every night when you turn on this little panda, I want you to imagine it's me sitting right there beside you, watching over you, making sure you feel safe and loved. When the world goes quiet and it's just you and the soft glow of this lamp, know that somewhere out there, I'm thinking of you too.

You know, pandas also remind me of us — they're a beautiful mix of black and white, just like how our differences come together to create something perfectly balanced . We complement each other in ways I never thought possible.

So let this panda be your nighttime companion, your tiny guardian, your little piece of me that stays with you always. May its glow remind you that no matter how dark the night gets, my love for you never dims.

You are my warmth, my light, my adorable panda. 🐼💛

Your Forever Night Light,
~ Athreya 💛
