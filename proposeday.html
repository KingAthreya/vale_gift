Your Butterfly Ring

Mi Mariposa,

I chose this butterfly ring for you because butterflies remind me of you - of transformation, of beauty, and of the delicate yet resilience in your heart. It is also a reminder of our struggles and how far we have come together and how much we have to learn and understand each other.

The purple and silver remind me of twilight - that enchanting moment when the world is caught between day and night, when everything feels possible and time seems to stand still. That's exactly how I feel when I'm with you - like I'm suspended in the most beautiful moment, wishing it would never end.

You are my butterfly - graceful, mesmerizing, and free. Yet somehow, you chose to land beside me, to share your colors with my world, to make my ordinary days extraordinary. And for that, I am endlessly grateful.

On this Propose Day, I want you to know that I don't just love you for who you are - I love who I become when I'm with you. You are the best gift I could ask for and how much greatful i am to have you in my life.

Every time you look at this ring, remember: you are not just wearing jewelry - you are carrying a piece of my heart, a symbol of my promise to always cherish you, protect you, and be there for you.

You are my butterfly, my twilight, my mariposa. 🦋💜

Always by your side,
~ Athreya 💜
